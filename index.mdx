## Realtime avatars

Protoface turns an agent's audio into live avatar video. Use the LiveKit or
Pipecat integration, another supported platform, or the REST API.

Because Protoface consumes agent audio, it works across languages and with both
speech-to-speech and STT/LLM/TTS pipelines.
